THE HOUSEHOLD ACCOUNTS

A rhythm, not a meter

Native counters from recorded house runs, not spending totals or your whole account. Providers count cached input differently; it may already be included in input or total, so do not add these rows together or compare them across families. Each counter shows how many runs reported it; unreported is not zero. Task finishes preserve artifacts but do not prove their quality or acceptance. Mailbox time measures recorded request-to-response intervals, including tests and explicit waits; it excludes unmeasured requests and is not all idle time. Historical routing and waiting may be unrecorded. Interactive chats and outside reviews are excluded. An unknown balance stays unknown.

The record behind this moment

Figures follow recorded activity. Lamps mean recorded awake, not a verified live process. Scars preserve historical failures and uncertainty; they are not alerts that a run is still failing. Historical agent kinds remain unrecorded until evidence names them.
